# Technical ML experiment, registered before fitting

User objective: train on market data, trends, charts and indicators to seek positive returns after costs, retaining the existing capital and risk policy. A trained model is not evidence of guaranteed profit. Scope: reproducible offline training and realistic option replay; no live order activation.

Data and chronological roles:

- Training: existing 120 observed development sessions in January–June 2025 (Q1/Q2 robustness datasets).
- Validation/model selection: 60 July–September 2025 sessions (Q3).
- Evaluation after selection is frozen: 60 October–December 2025 sessions (Q4), then the existing 60 January–April 2026 development sessions. These dates were seen in earlier rule experiments; they are held out from this model's fit/selection, not claimed as a pristine final confirmation sample.
- Reserved 24 April–21 July 2026 remains excluded from features, labels, fitting, model selection and outcome replay. No random row split. Labels exit within their own session, so whole-session splits prevent labels crossing boundaries.

Inputs: numerical closed-candle features, not chart screenshots. Underlying: normalized returns, EMA trend/slope, MACD, RSI, ATR, ADX/directional movement, Bollinger position/width, stochastic position, candle body/wicks, rolling breakout distances, trend efficiency, realized volatility, session range/open and prior-session levels. Option: observed returns, ATR, RSI, actual traded volume, relative volume and session VWAP; contract type, moneyness, DTE and time of day. No fabricated NIFTY index volume. No future-fitted transforms or full-period normalization.

Target: realized counterfactual net P&L / planned risk for an eligible single-lot long option entered next minute. Same observed-contract eligibility and volatility-aware 10% minimum stop / 2R target as filtered replay; observed minute exits, adverse slippage and costs, stop-first ambiguity. This is a trade label, not a portfolio return. Missing future prices produce an unavailable label, never zero. Features and opportunity generation do not depend on outcome availability. Actual held-out portfolio results retain shared two-bucket, whole-lot, cash-buffer and persistent drawdown admission.

Compare exactly two regressors: Ridge(alpha=10) with training-fitted scaling, and HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_iter=120, max_leaf_nodes=7, min_samples_leaf=100, learning_rate=0.05, l2_regularization=10, early_stopping=False, random_state=42). No internal random early-stopping validation. Clip training targets to [-3,3] R; keep evaluation labels and P&L unaltered. Weight observations so each training session contributes equal total weight. Fixed score thresholds 0.10, 0.20, 0.30 R. Choose highest stressed validation P&L among complete candidates with at least 20 trades; ties prefer base P&L then deterministic model/threshold order. If none qualifies, select the highest stressed candidate as a diagnostic only and retain failed status. Freeze before inspecting evaluation outcomes. No search expansion after failures.

Validation and evaluation: actual option replay under base 10 bps slippage/zero brokerage and stress 30 bps/₹20 per order with the existing hypothetical current Kotak fee schedule. At each observed time select the higher-scoring eligible CE/PE only if above threshold. Preserve filtered execution safeguards, 15-minute cooldown and three entries/day. Compare with unchanged filtered-rule baseline and no-trade benchmark. Replay evaluation periods separately and as one continuous account without an intervening capital reset.

Evidence screen: positive base and stressed validation with >=20 validation trades; positive base and stressed results in both later periods with >=40 base trades combined; complete outcomes, zero ambiguous exits; positive net improvement over the rule baseline. Report all candidate validation results, turnover, drawdown, rejected entries and feature importance. A failed screen remains failed; no deployment or final holdout consumption.

Implementation: causal feature/label module, offline CLI and saved model/feature/configuration/data fingerprints; optional hash-bound research signal schedule in existing replay so risk decisions stay in the shared core. The schedule is not accepted as a live strategy or public API payload. Regression tests cover future-price invariance, next-bar labels, missing quotes, chronological separation, and schedule integrity. Models and datasets remain local and ignored by Git. Existing production behavior without a schedule must remain identical.
