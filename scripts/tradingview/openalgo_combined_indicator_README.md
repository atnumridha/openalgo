# OpenAlgo combined TradingView indicator

Source: `openalgo_combined_indicator.pine`, Pine Script v6. Use standard candles and a chart with OHLC history. All primary studies are enabled by default. Neutral candle observations are optional.

## What appears

- Price chart: custom SMA-seeded EMA9 (blue), EMA15 (red), EMA21 (violet).
- Large green/red X: first qualifying three-EMA Long/Short alignment after a witnessed regime change. Requires strict ordering, adjacent gaps of at least 0.1 ATR, matching three-bar slopes, and close beyond all three. This is an alignment event; all three pairwise crossings need not occur on the same candle.
- Small green/red dots: ordinary EMA9/15 crossovers, separate from the three-EMA X.
- Candle LONG/SHORT labels: one main name per direction on each completed candle, dotted leader and arrow-style label. Hover for meaning and all deduplicated matches. Default text size is 12 points (adjustable from 10–20), with a solid dark background. Direction and pattern name occupy separate lines; nearby labels move outward to reduce crowding. Spacing uses ATR, so extreme zoom levels can still crowd annotations. Different direction observations can coexist.
- RSI+MACD LONG/SHORT: RSI14 leaving 30/70, with a matching MACD12/26/9 cross within five bars and a growing directional histogram. One setup per RSI exit; independent of candle/EMA labels.
- Gautam Jha 1H sweep reversal, enabled by default: previous UTC-day high/low in amber; an optional amber pending sweep label (hidden by default); green `GJ LONG` or red `GJ SHORT` after its immediate next 1H candle closes with matching colour and a strict sweep-boundary break. Hover for the entry reference, trigger, SL, target and rule explanation. Pending and confirmed observations use dotted leaders. Latest reference/SL/target guides are labelled separately from any broker order.
- Fibonacci P, R1–R3, S1–S3 from previous completed anchor-period HLC. Ratios: 0.382, 0.618, 1.0. Four pivot periods retained by default. Auto anchor is daily intraday, weekly on daily charts, monthly above daily. Select 240 for 4H pivots. An anchor below the chart interval is suppressed.
- Classical patterns, enabled by default: one newest confirmed complete formation outline, with boundary/close reference, projected target where defined and structural invalidation. The bottom-left guide names the shape and next condition. Unconfirmed classical geometry is optional and off by default. See the live-pattern section below.
- Lower pane: RSI14, 30/50/70 guides, plus relative-volume bars in the 0–20 band. Volume/SMA20 is capped at 3× for display. Raw volume, relative volume and MACD values are in the Data Window. Volume may represent ticks depending on the TradingView feed.

“Fibonacci pivots (4)” was interpreted as four retained pivot periods, adjustable in Inputs. It does not mean four R/S levels or a mandatory 4H anchor.

## Timing and scope

EMA lines update with the chart's current price. Discrete Xs, dots, candle and RSI+MACD observations appear only when the chart candle closes. The indicator follows the selected chart symbol/timeframe; it does not import the OpenAlgo Auto scalper's separate 1m/5m broker eligibility logic. TradingView runs Pine calculations; this file cannot use the local OpenAlgo backend. Standard EMAs remain smoothing indicators and cannot be made mathematically lag-free.

Confirmed higher-period pivots use `[1]` with `lookahead_on` to avoid future-period HLC leakage. Different data vendors, loaded starting history and sessions can produce different EMA values and pattern observations. Candle pattern rules are local operational definitions ported from this project, not a profitability claim. Flat/malformed history is quarantined for the needed rolling window, then recognition recovers.

## Install or recover

1. Open TradingView → Pine Editor → Create new → Indicator.
2. Replace the starter text with the complete `.pine` file.
3. Add to chart; save privately if the account has a free script slot.
4. For existing EMA/candle/RSI alerts, choose Once Per Bar Close. For the new Gautam 1H conditions, use Once Per Bar: the source 1H confirmation is already closed, and waiting for the viewing chart's candle to close adds an unnecessary delay.

## Gautam Jha screenshot setup (4 November update)

The user selected **Gautam Jha only**. No 4H PO3/FVG engine was added.

The 1H module is independent of EMA, RSI, MACD and trend eligibility. A Long requires a green hourly candle below the preceding UTC day's low, followed immediately by a green hour whose high strictly breaks that sweep high. A Short mirrors this at the previous-day high. Both candles must fit in the fixed **12:30–21:30 IST** window. A sweep in the final hour cannot arm an impossible confirmation outside that window. One confirmed observation is allowed per direction per UTC day; this is a signal count, not an actual trade count.

If an hour invalidates the preceding pending setup but independently meets the sweep rules, it can arm a fresh setup for its own immediate next hour. The failed preceding setup does not create an entry.

The supplied notes simultaneously require the next candle's final colour and entry during its wick break. The approved implementation waits for the next hour to close, then uses that **close as the entry reference**. The trigger boundary is retained in the tooltip. It does not retroactively claim an entry at the earlier intrabar break. The stop is one minimum tick beyond the sweep wick; target is the opposite previous-day extreme. Confirmation bars that cross the stop or touch the target are skipped. Sweeps of both daily extremes in one hour are skipped because OHLC cannot establish their order.

UTC previous-day levels are assembled from 24 consecutive valid 1H candles starting at UTC midnight. Partial starting days or missing hours do not supply invented levels. This specifically suits continuous crypto feeds; instruments with daily trading breaks can show `Previous UTC day incomplete`. The fixed IST window is a source rule and is not described as a daylight-saving-adjusted London/New York overlap.

On charts at or below 1H, `[1]` with `lookahead_on` returns the completed hourly packet at the start of the next hour. On charts above 1H, all available closed hourly intrabars are processed. Source close timestamps prevent duplicated labels. A higher chart's intrabar availability remains subject to TradingView account/history limits. Transformed candles are unsupported by this module. It cannot guarantee tick-by-tick or millisecond delivery beyond the data feed.

Pattern labels appear only on the **last 10 chart candles by default**, including the forming candle slot. Older labels expire as the chart advances, even when there is no new pattern. Enable **Labels → Show patterns on all historical candles** to display older observations within the retention limits. Up to twenty confirmed GJ labels are retained, adjustable to 40. Existing candle-label retention is capped when needed to reserve room within Pine's drawing limit. Inputs separately control pending labels, guides, daily levels and the small status display. **Gautam Jha → Show unconfirmed sweeps and pending guides** defaults off. Hiding pending sweeps preserves the latest confirmed guides. GJ labels are dated when the source confirmation becomes available; a dotted leader points back to the source candle. This lets a newly confirmed hourly observation appear in the recent window on 1m/5m charts. When a target equals PDH/PDL, one caption names both levels. Confirmed historical guides say `last` so they do not imply an active broker position or a new entry at the current price.

Validation uses the actual Pine engine via the existing independent PineTS runtime, plus a replay of public Delta BTCUSD candles. Synthetic rule checks and market-candle identification are separate from profitability testing. None of the screenshots' XAUUSD backtest performance claims were reproduced or assigned to BTC.

## Live classical patterns and drawings (5 November)

**Classical chart patterns → Classical chart patterns + geometry** and the explanatory guide are on by default. The layer ports the app's29 classical names and four short-pattern names: NR4 breakout, inside-bar breakout, pipe bottom and pipe top. It draws the observed formation geometry and current reference levels, rather than a name alone. **Show unconfirmed classical geometry** defaults off; enabled pending drawings are amber/dashed and never count as a confirmed direction. The shared **Show patterns on all historical candles** option controls retained older geometry; its default is off. The recent-ten window includes the forming candle slot. At most one newest complete formation is drawn by default to keep the chart readable.

The guide refreshes with chart executions and shows the expected direction, next completed closing/retest condition, measured projection where defined and structural invalidation. A current candle's direction/countdown and provisional boundary position can change intrabar; confirmed pattern history changes at close. A later verified stop/target touch, ambiguous both-reference candle or close back through the boundary retires a reference. No earlier wick in the confirming candle is treated as a post-confirmation exit. NR4/inside-bar have no invented measured target. Reference levels are not broker orders or assumed fills.

Classical fits use strict confirmed2-left/2-right pivots and the app's restrictive local numerical rules. The engine fits up to180 observations, evaluates the latest1500 source candles and retains at most80 confirmations. It supports standard continuous intraday OHLC. Missing intervals restart its source segment; daily/transformed charts show unavailable. Missing native volume is normalized to zero at this geometry-only boundary. A full data-feed update cannot be guaranteed every millisecond.

OpenAlgo uses **Live pattern guide** in Trend & setup. Its existing **Pattern help** controls hide unconfirmed and older annotations by default; outlines now also work on index and option charts. Quotations update the lightweight candle guidance; heavy detection stays on verified candle history. Sources and explicitly local settings are available under **Pattern references & rules**. The changing TradingView ideas feed is not imported as executable rules, and Dhan's unavailable article body is not claimed as reviewed.
