# Chart auto trading, recovery, manual entries and trade alerts

Current user choices: default50x leverage/100% available-margin sizing (editable); automatic trend entry, protection/trailing and exit; resume Auto after verified reconciliation; attempt a scalper-window exit without forcing a strong protected winner out; persistent on-screen alerts and sounds; manual maker entries with managed SL/trailing.

- Initial Auto activation is a user action. The exact account/symbol/interval, sizing settings and arming generation persist in browser local storage. Reload restores only after fresh account/orders/pending intents/managed-plan reconciliation. An open trade requires exactly acknowledged owned full-size market SL and maker target. Foreign or unknown exposure remains paused.
- OFF revokes the saved generation. Cross-tab storage events and checks under the account/product Web Lock, after fresh preflight and immediately before POST reject old authorizations. OFF remains available during broker or candle-history outages by pinning account/interval scope. Sizing controls freeze while armed; turn OFF to edit them. An already-sent request must reconcile.
- Browser localStorage claims one attempt per account/symbol/interval/closed candle. A Web Lock spans automatic preflight and submission. Server UUID intents and product/account locks remain authoritative; unknown outcomes never receive a blind retry.
- Explicit Manual order direction skips frontend trend approval. Long uses a fresh best bid, Short a fresh best offer, both post-only. Following explicit user approval, manual entries also skip the sharp-candle and directional chase/pullback vetoes. Auto retains both vetoes. Freshness, valid OHLC/ATR, closed-breakout, margin/fee, liquidation and protection validation remains. Manual entries require a managed SL/target and retain fee-aware trailing; they do not automatically exit merely because their manually chosen side differs from the trend. No other server checks are bypassed.
- The live chart performs new entries, trailing, trend exits and soft scalper reviews. Existing acknowledged exchange stops/targets stay active when the chart closes. The backend independently reconciles owned fills and restores fixed protection; it does not invent entries or directional trailing while the browser is closed.
- The soft review begins30seconds before the official symbol window, anchored to the first verified owned entry fill (persisted during management, never from order placement). A strong aligned net-positive trade continues only with an acknowledged full-size reduce-only market SL already covering verified entry plus estimated taker exit costs. Otherwise it requests the existing reduce-only market exit. Exit timing/fills/discount are not guaranteed.
- BTCUSD/ETHUSD use30minutes; other eligible futures15minutes; XAUTUSD/PAXGUSD/SLVONUSD have no discount-based exit. Offer opt-in/terms remain Delta's; fee estimates retain ordinary costs until actual fill evidence proves otherwise.
- Typed trade alerts distinguish accepted orders, entry fills, partial reductions and verified full exits. They persist until manual dismissal, including reload, and sound once per new event. A delayed-fill transition is retained up to60seconds; broker ACK alone never proves a fill. Sound can require a browser gesture after reload. No Telegram/email/external messages are added.
- Trend eligibility is unchanged. LiveBTC evidence showed rising5m EMAs with lower confirmed swing highs/lows, which correctly blocks combined confirmation. The UI now displays exact failed EMA/swing conditions instead of a generic repeated rejection.

Verification:254 targeted frontend/chart tests,236 backend lifecycle tests, TypeScript and production build. Broker calls are mocked in tests. Live chart/account inspection was read-only; no live order or Auto activation clicks by the agent.
