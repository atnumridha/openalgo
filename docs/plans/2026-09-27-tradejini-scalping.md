# Tradejini options scalping: frozen offline experiment

Source read 27 September 2026: https://www.tradejini.com/blogs/introduction-to-scalping-in-nifty-options (Suchitra, 11 June 2025). This experiment tests explicit adaptations, not the discretionary article verbatim. Earlier studies used EMA5/10 or EMA9/15 and index-based stops; neither these EMA9/21 rules nor these native option-premium barriers has been evaluated.

## Scope fixed before outcomes

Twelve cells: EMA9/21 and opening-box breakout, each on 1/3-minute charts, each with maximum holding time 5/10/15 minutes. Four 10-minute cells are primary. No parameter selection or retraining after looking at returns. Both CE and PE are bought, never sold short. Use a 10-point premium stop and 20-point target (gross 1:2), not the article's erroneous stop above a bought put's entry. Select the article's fixed-target alternative; do not add EMA exits, partial exits or trailing. No new live/sandbox strategy activation, orders, broker requests or configuration changes.

Use the established observed NIFTY spot minute history, 2025-01-01 through 2026-04-23, excluding 2025-10-21 Muhurat session before features. Protected Apr24–Jul21 outcomes are not accessed. The isolated 2025-08-23 observation remains in indicator history; a three-consecutive-bar gate prevents entries after gaps. Futures volume for this period is unavailable locally; inspected Zenodo futures ZIP covers only 2017–2020. VWAP is untested. Box results omit the article's essential volume confirmation. Signals use spot as a proxy for the illustrated futures chart. No delta, news or bid/ask-spread confirmation is available.

## Causal signal definitions

- Build complete 1/3-minute OHLC candles anchored at 09:15 IST and labelled by close; never fill missing minutes. Indicators carry overnight, but require three consecutive chart intervals after any gap. Only signals whose full holding deadline is no later than 15:25 can enter.
- EMA9/21: first-close seed, adjust=False, 105 observed chart bars warm-up. Compare fast-minus-slow rounded to eight decimals. Fresh upward cross plus close above both EMAs and bullish body at least 50% of candle range buys CE; downward mirror buys PE. All regular-session signals are eligible. This body threshold and warm-up are declared research assumptions; volume and optional VWAP are omitted.
- Box: require every constituent minute of 09:15–09:30, high/low over these 15 minutes, width >=20 index points. Consider the first closed chart candle outside the box after 09:30 and through 09:45. Require a directional body >=50% of range. If that first breakout is weak, skip the day rather than wait for a favourable second attempt. Do not use the optional, unspecified high-volatility second window. Range bars can never signal themselves.

## Contract selection and execution

At each signal close select one ATM NIFTY option lot: earliest eligible expiry 0–7 calendar days, then minimum absolute strike distance from spot, then lower strike to break an exact tie. Use only contracts in the latest preceding NSE listing (no older than seven days), with its lot size, and a completed five-minute option quote at the floor-five-minute boundary with positive premium and volume >=10 lots. Quotes may be 0–4 minutes old. No fallback to a cheaper contract when admission fails. No observed delta requirement; ATM is a strike-distance proxy.

Enter at the next minute's open boundary (the signal close timestamp) with adverse tick-rounded slippage. Entry premium plus buy fees must fit ₹20,000 (₹25,000 capital with 20% buffer). Refuse a planned stop loss including both modeled fees and stop-exit slippage above ₹1,000. A gap can still lose more. Stop is filled entry minus10, target filled entry plus20. Shared services/risk core judges long-option breaches. Check each option minute sequentially; a gap through stop fills at its worse open, a gap above target receives only target, and when both barriers are touched after a between-barriers open assume stop first. Resting barriers are assumed executable with adverse slippage; OHLC cannot prove fills or spreads. Time exit at deadline's option open, with no extra minute. Require valid OHLC and positive volume for every used bar; do not condition an early exit on subsequent path availability. Record intrabar exits at that bar's close conservatively for scheduling. Opening-gap/time exits use boundary timestamp.

Independently per cell and cost scenario: at most three admitted option entries per day, no overlap. Failed entry/contract/funding/risk checks do not consume a trade slot. A missing post-entry bar consumes a slot and locks further entry through that trade's maximum deadline; report it as unresolved, never as zero P&L. Base:10bp slippage each side and zero brokerage; stress:30bp and ₹20/order plus configured exchange/SEBI/GST/stamp/STT. Scenario entry fills can change barriers and later admission, so sample counts may differ.

These are initial-capital opportunity tests, not a compounded portfolio. The first-trade/later-trade daily loss buckets, ₹2,000 daily loss pause and portfolio drawdown are NOT simulated. Publish this limitation. Fees are modeled assumptions, not historical broker bills. Exact net1:2 is not promised.
