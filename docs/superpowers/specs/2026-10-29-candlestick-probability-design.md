# Candlestick outcome probability

Build a separate, symbol-independent price-pattern study in the running OpenAlgo dashboard and chart. The same geometry and price-context rules apply to every symbol; empirical evidence is isolated by symbol, exchange and timeframe. Never transfer NIFTY statistics to BTC, Sensex or an option contract.

User-selected outcome: the chart price reaches a 2R target before its stop within the next ten completed candles. This is a historical estimate of that event, not option profitability or an 80% guarantee. Default qualification: at least 50 resolved matching observations and a Laplace-smoothed estimate of at least 80%. Show the raw successes/count and Wilson 95% interval. Below threshold or insufficient evidence means WAIT.

Fixed, price-only pattern families: body engulfing; long-wick rejection; inside-bar breakout. Bullish/bearish geometry is symmetric. Prior ten-bar price movement divided by ten-bar ATR gives aligned, opposing or flat context (0.5 ATR boundary). Resolve simultaneous patterns in that fixed order. No volume requirement and no symbol-specific entry rule. Entry reference is the pattern closing price. Stop is beyond the pattern extreme by 0.1 ATR, with at least 0.5 ATR distance; target is twice that distance. Support/resistance are the prior ten completed candles' low/high.

Only complete, valid and contiguous candles count. Reset context at gaps and, for Indian intraday markets, each session. Continuous markets cross midnight normally. Require ten future scheduled bars before Indian session end. Do not promote a cached forming candle merely because wall time advances. Outcome evaluation starts after the pattern candle. If stop and target touch in one bar, count stop conservatively; timeout is a failure to reach 2R, not automatically a losing trade. Missing bars invalidate a pending outcome. Use non-overlapping reference observations; counts update only when the outcome is known. Unresolved cases never enter counts.

Historical models carry exact market scope, rule version and training cutoff. No prediction at or before that cutoff may use the seed. Runtime samples are subsequent to it. All predictions retain their creation-time estimate. Offline testing uses the same TypeScript engine as the product with chronological evaluation; no tuning thresholds after seeing results. NIFTY has audited Kotak history; other markets learn from their own loaded chart candles and show limited evidence honestly.

Dashboard: separate card, selected index/timeframe, decision, pattern, probability, sample count, interval, entry/SL/2R/support/resistance references and last outcome. Chart: selectable Candlestick probability study with markers and price levels, enabled independently of nine-indicator vote. CALL/PUT denotes bullish/bearish underlying direction on an index. On non-index charts label bullish/bearish instead of implying that an option or contract exists. On an option chart these are premium-direction signals, not a recommendation to buy the opposite option. Confirmed signals only; current feed error/stale candle suppresses actionable decisions. No orders.

Sources for pattern geometry: Schwab/thinkorswim Engulfing documentation and TradingView automatic candlestick detection documentation. Neither supplies probabilities; compute them from source data. Pattern evidence and evaluation results must remain visible even when zero setups qualify at 80%.
