## Frozen signals and data timing

All timestamps denote completed candles in IST. EMA is tested on one-minute candles as the source specifies; the four other interpretations use five-minute candles. Five-minute candles require every constituent minute. No fill-forward. EMA, RSI and Bollinger histories count observed bars and carry overnight; require three consecutive intervals before a signal after a gap/session boundary. RSI pivots and support/range lookbacks reset at each gap/session boundary. Futures, Bank Nifty, news and option-selling strategies are outside this study.

1. **EMA crossover (`ema`)**: EMA spans 5 and 10, recursive adjust=False, seed first close; wait at least 50 observed closes. Call on a completed upward crossover (previous fast<=slow, current fast>slow), put on the reverse. No trend/volume or opposite-crossover exit added.
2. **RSI divergence (`divergence`)**: Wilder RSI14 seeded from the first 14 changes. A swing low/high must be strictly below/above both of the two neighboring bars on each side. It is confirmed only two bars later; entries occur after confirmation, never at the pivot. Compare consecutive confirmed lows/highs in the same uninterrupted intraday segment, separated by 5–60 bars. Bullish divergence means lower price low but higher RSI at the lows; bearish means higher high but lower RSI. Both RSI readings must be finite. No threshold/size filter. Simultaneous bullish/bearish signals are skipped.
3. **Bollinger squeeze (`squeeze`)**: SMA20 plus/minus two population standard deviations. Bandwidth=(upper-lower)/middle. Squeeze requires bandwidth <= the 10th percentile of the preceding 120 widths, excluding current. Breakout closes above the previous upper band with previous close<=that band (reverse below lower). At least one squeeze must have occurred in the five preceding bars. Require those five intervals contiguous within the session; no volume confirmation, so this is not the separately mentioned volume breakout.
4. **Pin-bar level rejection (`pinbar`)**: prior 20 uninterrupted intraday candles define support=min(low), resistance=max(high); exclude current candle. Bullish candle touches/breaches support and closes back above it, body<=one-third of total range, lower wick>=twice body and >=half range, upper wick<=one-quarter range, close>open. Bearish mirror at resistance. Zero-range bars do not qualify.
5. **Range bounce (`range`)**: same prior-20 levels, prior Wilder ATR14 (recursive seed from first 14 true ranges). Prior range width must be <=4*ATR. Bullish candle touches/breaches support, closes above support but below range midpoint, close>open; bearish mirror above midpoint at resistance. This is a declared compact-range interpretation, not a parameter supplied by the author.

All five use a common signal-close stop 15 index points away, following the guide's broad 10–20-point index-stop suggestion. This is an added comparative assumption, not a premium or rupee stop. Entry is the next minute open; reject entry through stop. Target=2*actual entry-to-stop distance. Stop first when both stop/target touched; shared pure risk evaluator decides breaches. No indicator exit. Time caps 5,10,15 minutes; primary cap 15 for each family, total 15 variants. Full horizon must end by 15:25. Limit to three serial index entries/day for comparability with the earlier IG study, not the article's suggested high trade frequency. Scheduling does not reserve capital for the additional option exit-delay minute.

This tests independent opportunities at fixed initial affordability. It does not compound equity, enforce daily loss buckets or an option-premium stop.
