- Standalone signal rules: green labels read BUY CALL, red labels BUY PUT, WAIT is yellow. IV requires two fresh matched CE/PE IV comparisons: only CE IV falling gives CALL; only PE falling gives PUT; both/neither gives WAIT. This app-defined pricing comparison does not establish price direction and is disclosed. PCR below1 gives CALL, above1 gives PUT, equal1 gives WAIT, using actual quoted-window put/call volume. OI uses nearest nonexpired underlying futures: rising own OI with rising own price gives CALL, rising OI with falling price gives PUT; other cases WAIT. No MA bias or other indicator confirms these rows. Missing/expired options expiry affects IV/PCR only; valid futures OI/MFI can continue. Each feed carries its own observation timestamp and freshness guard.
- MFI uses chart volume where present; on an index it may use the nearest underlying future's real OHLCV as a clearly labelled proxy. Missing proxy stays unavailable. All CALL/PUT labels apply to underlying index direction; require Nifty/BankNifty/Sensex index charts rather than mislabelling option-premium or crypto signals.
- Show all enabled studies in a compact on-chart panel. Each has a numeric value, last-event or context label and observation time. Neutral/initializing/unavailable are explicit grey states. Indicators operate independently; do not combine their votes into a trading recommendation.
- Nine persisted checkboxes under the chart's Options controls. Selected option identity and data freshness travel with context. Disable live option context during replay, saved ranges, crypto, and when chart/underlying references do not match.
- Article is qualitative: periods and exact cross rules above are implementation choices, disclosed in the panel's rule guide. No new order or strategy routes.
