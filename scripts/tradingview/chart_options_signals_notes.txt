AUTOMATIC MARKET IDENTIFICATION
NSE:NIFTY, NSE:BANKNIFTY and BSE:SENSEX, plus their recognized dated C/P option
tickers. The table identifies the chart and underlying. Similar names such as
NIFTYBEES and NIFTY100 do not silently map to NIFTY. Underlying prices select
strikes; option-chart premiums are used only to position labels. Underlying
strike-price plots are suppressed on option charts.

CONFIGURING ANOTHER MARKET OR CYCLE
Enable Use configured cycle below. Bind Configured underlying to the detected
market, set exact entry/expiry times and timezone/session, then supply the ATM
and leg-bank centres. Use the strike interval supported by that expiry; preset
intervals are 50 for NIFTY and 100 for BANKNIFTY/SENSEX. An explicit interval
can override them for a configured cycle, including fractional increments.
For SD, configure both hedge-bank centres and the trading-session horizon.
For ATR, supply that market's 5-week ATR or continuous future feed, and a
prior-day annualized volatility percentage or volatility-index feed. NIFTY's
pinned ATR and India VIX are never silently used for a different market.

Verify the option symbol template against an actual TradingView contract.
Tokens: {root}, {expiry}, {side}, {strike}. Default NSE-style template is
{root}{expiry}{side}{strike}, date yyMMdd, side C/P. Custom roots must include
the exchange. BSE and other markets require verification of their actual feed
and contract format before configuration. Missing symbols produce WAIT/no
entry rather than an invalid-symbol runtime failure.

Only European cash-settled options quoted in underlying price units fit the
current pricing model. Configured profiles require acknowledgment of this
condition. American exercise, inverse/coin-denominated premiums, and physical
delivery need different modelling and are not validated by this adaptation.
For other markets bind an exact underlying ID. To display on another option
chart, also bind that exact chart ID and its verified expiry/settlement time.
Changing the configured cycle without matching that expiry blocks signals.

DATA COVERAGE
Fixed banks request at most 39 contexts per indicator, including base feeds.
ATR scans 15 strikes per side plus the three-strike ATM bank; SD retains four
eight-strike leg banks plus ATM. Adjust bank centres when required strikes fall
outside coverage. New strike contexts are not added during realtime execution.
Underlying source-bar sequence detects missing option-chart candles even over
session boundaries. Missing required observations give REVIEW - DATA GAP;
this state is persistent through the open cycle. The script cannot infer a
stop fill from unseen bars. Holidays do not count as gaps when the underlying
also has no bars. Custom feeds with different sessions may conservatively
require review. A chart with sparse bars can therefore differ in coverage
status from the underlying chart while retaining the same entry legs.

VALIDATION
No historical win-rate claim transfers to other markets. Non-NIFTY profiles
are explicitly labelled ADAPTED RULES - NOT BACKTESTED. Synthetic tests prove
routing/math behavior, not market profitability or available option liquidity.
Signals and quoted credits are not broker fills; no orders are submitted.
