CHART ENTRY / EXIT + SUGGESTED SL + SUPPORT / RESISTANCE

This is a separate research indicator for the exact symbol on the chart. It does not read, modify or protect a broker position. A blue broker position line can coexist with independent signal-trade levels.

Read the chart:
- Cyan SUPPORT and orange RESISTANCE: nearest confirmed swing levels. A swing becomes available only after its right-side confirmation bars. Levels change as price and confirmed swings change.
- Yellow WAIT / RETEST: no entry yet. A resistance breakout must close above a rising EMA21, then a later bullish candle must retest the broken level within 8 bars and close back above it.
- Green BUY / ENTRY: a confirmed entry reference on that exact chart. On CE and PE charts alike, this means buying the premium; it does not select the opposite option.
- Red SL: fixed suggested stop below the retest low/broken resistance, with an ATR/tick buffer.
- Green TARGET: up to 2R, capped below a known resistance. A setup with less than 1.5R room is rejected.
- EXIT label: target touched, SL touched, session close, gap below SL, ambiguous range, or missing-data review. SL and target touch reporting occurs at candle close. It is not an immediately active protective broker order.

Defaults are intraday standard candles. NSE/BSE: entries 09:20 to 15:00 IST; session exit at the first bar closing at/after 15:15. Other exchanges: continuous intraday entries, with gaps ending signal tracking if outcomes cannot be established. Larger chart intervals mean later exit reporting. Historical fills are not promised.

If WAIT or MARKET CLOSED is displayed, there is no new entry instruction. Historical signals remain visible. Entry/SL/target fields are blank when there is no active signal trade. Existing broker positions are not automatically adopted.
