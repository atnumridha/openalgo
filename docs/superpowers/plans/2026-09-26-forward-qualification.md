# Forward qualification and reviewed live release

The user explicitly requested full implementation of the missing paper qualification and live-release workflow. This continues the previously approved capital/research design. Work stays on the existing feature checkout with its uncommitted foundation; no live orders, account activation or deployment is implied.

## Design

A prospective paper campaign binds a completed sealed-final research reference to the exact managed strategy, linked Flow graph, pinned broker account, cost schedule, capital policy and execution-source fingerprint. Historical simulations are screening evidence; actual forward qualification measures the bound strategy/flow that will execute live. The UI must distinguish the research reference from evidence of that deployed configuration. A strategy configuration change starts a new campaign, not a selective reset of old trades.

Campaign enrollment does not submit orders. Existing automated Sandbox Strategy Module execution continues through the same governor. Every admitted campaign trade is registered before dispatch; order quotes are captured server-side from the authenticated pinned broker and saved before dispatch; cumulative fills and terminal outcomes are copied automatically from the durable risk ledger. No public endpoint can upload a P&L number or fabricated paper fill. Exits always remain possible when qualification storage/quotes fail; the evidence becomes incomplete and cannot qualify.

Paper P&L uses conservative executable prices (BUY no better than ask, SELL no better than bid), the explicit per-order schedule and adverse slippage. Each filled order needs native timestamped bid/ask, sufficient quoted quantity, matching broker/account/symbol and bounded quote-to-fill delay. Partial/rejected/unknown/unbalanced orders remain visible. The evidence registry is durable and bounded; corrections invalidate prior reconciliation and approval. No broker fee is claimed to have been charged for a Sandbox simulation.

Qualification policy v1 requires a complete, profitable sealed final screen (at least 20 trades), and at least 30 distinct forward trading sessions plus 100 closed trades, positive net expectancy, profit factor >=1.2, positive stressed net P&L and maximum observed marked drawdown <=15%. No unresolved position, invalid execution evidence, risk breach or stale configuration is allowed. These are engineering eligibility thresholds, not a promise of future returns. They are versioned, visible and cannot be weakened by an API request.

The operator reviews reconciliation and confirms the dated cost assumptions. A separate explicit approval with a reason binds the exact current evidence digest, campaign revision, strategy/flow/source/risk/cost/account version and broker authentication epoch. Approval expires after seven days; revocation is immediate. Daily live-session authorization and existing broker-held protective-stop checks remain independently required. Approval never toggles live_enabled, arms a Flow or submits an order. Live entries recheck the release immediately before dispatch; exits bypass it. New paper evidence, corrections, version drift, expired/revoked broker/session or risk pause closes the gate.

## Interfaces and ownership

- `services/research/qualification_context.py` (root): `current_binding(owner, strategy_id, strategy_config=None, costs=None) -> dict`; includes binding_hash, broker_connection_id, broker, broker_epoch, costs, strategy_hash, workflow_hash, source_hash, risk_policy_version. Never return tokens or workflow secrets. Stable campaign hash excludes authentication epoch; approval includes it.
- `database/strategy_qualification_db.py`, `services/risk/qualification.py`, `services/research/qualification.py` (backend agent): durable campaigns, prospective trade registrations, order quote receipts, reconciliation, approvals, pure performance/eligibility evaluation. Public service functions: `overview(owner)`, `create_campaign(owner, payload)`, `detail(owner, campaign_id)`, `reconcile(owner, campaign_id, payload)`, `approve(owner, campaign_id, payload)`, `revoke(owner, campaign_id, payload)`, `register_entry(owner, strategy_id, trade_ref)`, `record_quote(owner, trade_ref, order_id, quote)`, `record_trade(owner, trade_ref, risk_row)`, `live_release_reason(owner, strategy_id, strategy_config, risk_policy_version, costs)`. `get_store()` uses normal DATABASE_URL; store factory injectable in tests. Root wires schema to startup/required migration.
- `blueprints/strategy_qualification.py` (root): authenticated `/strategy/api/qualification` GET overview; POST `/campaigns` {strategy_id,final_run_id}; GET `/campaigns/<id>`; POST `/campaigns/<id>/reconcile` {reason,costs_confirmed:true,expected_revision,evidence_digest}; POST `/campaigns/<id>/approve` {reason,acknowledged:true,expected_revision,evidence_digest}; POST `/campaigns/<id>/revoke` {reason}. Response success/data or error/message. Overview includes campaigns, available strategies and policy. Campaign includes id,strategy_id,final_run_id,created_at,binding_hash,status,qualification:{eligible,checks:[{code,passed,message,actual,required}],metrics},approval:{status,expires_at,reason}|null, reconciliation, trades.
- `services/strategy_module/trading_budget.py`, engine/signals/order_dispatch integration (root): prospective registration at reserve, quote capture at actual dispatch seam with durable order id, automatic risk evidence sync and immediate dispatch recheck.
- Frontend agent: qualification panel in Research with campaign selection/enrollment, progress and reasons, reconciliation, explicit approve/revoke, expiry/invalidation display. No automatic live toggle.

## Review focus

Stale evidence racing approval; a reconnect changing broker authorization; edited Flow nodes or costs; a late fill changing a reconciled trade; unknown dispatch outcomes and missing quotes; caller-owned JSON injecting approvals or P&L; evidence captured before enrollment; deletion/reset preserving audit; unrelated exits blocked by a qualification error; no synthetic test result presented as real returns.

- No application restart, migration against the operating database, real paper campaign, account activation, live order, deployment or commit was performed. Startup and required upgrade paths install the new schema on deployment; the updated frontend bundle is built locally.
