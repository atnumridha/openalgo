## New question and reuse

Compare the SAME EMA5/10 rule at 1, 3 and 5-minute chart intervals, and compare opening/closing entry windows. This tests a limited interpretation of the article's timeframe/session advice, not an original StockGro strategy or a universal ranking. EMA spans are held constant in bars, so their clock-time lookbacks change with the chart interval. Hold duration is separately fixed at 15 minutes; retain the user's 2R target. Sub-two-minute holds, 1R/1.5R targets, market-regime claims, news, VWAP, Bank Nifty, FX and tick execution are outside this experiment.

Nine comparison cells: three intervals times three entry windows. Reuse the already completed corrected Tradetron one-minute/all-session/15-minute baseline; run only the eight missing cells. Never relabel the reused result as an independent replication. Hash its original registration, verification, result, index ledger, option ledgers and signal file; validate matching data/code/settings and exact one-minute signals before reuse.

## Frozen rules

- Use the corrected Tradetron sample: saved NIFTY minute closes 2025-01-01–2026-04-23, excluding 2025-10-21 Muhurat session before indicators. Preserve the observed-history caveat for the isolated 2025-08-23 minute; the readiness gate blocks entries there. Do not read protected Apr24–Jul21 outcomes. No new data or source alterations.
- Complete, session-aligned 1/3/5-minute candles only; missing constituent minutes are never filled. Use EMA5/10 adjust=False, seed first observed close, 50 observed bars warm-up. Compare fast-minus-slow rounded to eight decimals. CE on upward cross through zero, PE on downward cross. Carry indicator history overnight, but require three consecutive chart intervals after every gap/session boundary. No regime, confirmation or opposite-cross exit filter.
- Entry windows are defined in IST at the signal close/next-minute-open boundary: `all` 09:15<=time<15:30, `opening` 09:15<=time<10:15, `closing` 14:30<=time<15:30. Indicators use all available past bars, not only bars within the entry window. Filter signals BEFORE scheduling and daily-cap counting. Opening-window trades may exit after 10:15; these are entry windows, not forced exit boundaries.
- Every complete 15-minute horizon must end by 15:25; consequently closing entries stop at 15:10. Limit to three serial INDEX entries/day independently for each comparison cell. No simultaneous index positions. The all-session cell selects the first three eligible entries and therefore overweights early sessions; it is not an unbiased all-hours sample. Missing/unaffordable options still consume those index slots. Session cells overlap and are correlated.
- Index stop is 15 points from signal close. Execute next minute open, refuse entry through stop, target twice actual entry-to-stop distance. Existing shared risk core decides breaches; stop first if both barriers occur in a minute. Time exit at the 15-minute limit. No new exit rule.
- One long ITM NIFTY option lot: earliest eligible expiry (1–7 days), nearest ITM strike, prior-session NSE listing/lot data, completed five-minute quote with volume>=10 lots. For 1/3-minute signals quotes may be 0–4 minutes old. Entry premium plus entry fees <=₹20,000 from ₹25,000 and 20% buffer. No cheaper replacement. Option entry/exit at the next minute's open boundary corresponding to signal/index-exit close; no extra full minute is appended to the holding limit. Missing or nonpositive-volume minutes across the path make the observation unavailable.
- Base costs: 10bp adverse slippage per side, zero brokerage, configured exchange/SEBI/GST/stamp/STT. Stress: 30bp and ₹20/order. These are modeled charges, not historical bills. No changes to live or sandbox configuration, no orders or broker calls.
