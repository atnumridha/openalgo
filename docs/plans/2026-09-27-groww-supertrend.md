## Rules frozen before outcomes

Four variants: 2R/3R × maximum5/10-minute hold. Primary: **2R and10 minutes**, retaining the user's preferred reward/risk. These are underlying-index R targets, not guaranteed option-premium or net-R multiples. Both down/up signals buy ATM PE/CE respectively.

Minute OHLC cannot locate the option fill at a simultaneous intraminute index trigger. Test a declared **close-confirmed adaptation**: the completed touch candle forms the setup; ONLY the immediately following minute may confirm, by closing strictly above its high (CE) or below its low (PE). Enter the option at the following minute-open boundary. No entry on the setup candle, no use of the confirmation candle's earlier open, and no retrospective option fill at the index trigger price. Confirmation must retain trend colour and must not touch the opposite setup extreme. A later break is not an entry for that expired setup. New completed touch candles can form new setups. Candle body colour is unrestricted, as in the transcript's examples. These choices can miss fast moves shown in the video; results cannot establish exact intraminute-strategy performance.

Supertrend uses ATR10, multiplier3; Wilder ATR seeded by the first ten true ranges, first true range high-low, subsequent true range includes previous close. Follow TradingView's published final upper/lower band and direction recurrence (https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000634738-supertrend/), initial valid trend down. Carry indicator history across observed sessions. Require100 observed bars before touch setups and three consecutive minute intervals after gaps.
